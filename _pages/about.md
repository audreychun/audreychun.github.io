Hi! I'm Audrey, a research data scientist at Stanford Medicine. My work dwells at the intersection of AI, machine learning, and medicine.

education
======
I graduated with a double major in Computer Science and Data Science in May 2025 from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/). During my time at Cal, I had the pleasure of working at [Berkeley Artificial Intelligence Research](https://bair.berkeley.edu/) in the [Song Lab](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~yss/group.html) on cross-protein transfer learning for variant effect prediction, and taught Berkeley's [foundational data science course](https://github.com/data-8).

my work and research
======
Currently, I develop multimodal digital biomarkers for neuromuscular disease at [Stanford NeuroIGNITE](https://med.stanford.edu/neurology/divisions/neuromuscular/neuro-IGNITE.html). Previously, I worked on semi-supervised clustering methods to characterize  neuroanatomical heterogeneity in the [Medical Imaging and Data Science (MINDS) Lab](https://www.mir.wustl.edu/research/research-centers/computational-imaging-research-center-circ/labs/sotiras-lab/) at [WashU Medicine](https://medicine.wustl.edu/) (now part of [UPenn AIBIL](https://aibil.med.upenn.edu/)) , and the [Gevaert Lab](https://med.stanford.edu/gevaertlab.html) at [Stanford Medicine](https://med.stanford.edu/) on deep learning reconstructions of 3D digital breast tomosynthesis from 2D mammography, physiologically realistic image augmentation, and breast cancer foundation models.
